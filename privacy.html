Privacy policy

Hattrick Manager Club (mobile) · last updated 15 August 2026 · Español

Hattrick Manager Club is a free, read-only CHPP app that shows a Hattrick manager the current state of their own club. This policy explains exactly what data it handles, why, for how long, and what you can do about it.

Who is responsible

Hattrick Manager Club is developed by a single independent Hattrick user. It is not operated by Hattrick Holdings Limited, and Hattrick takes no responsibility for it.

The short version

There is no server. The app talks to Hattrick directly from your phone. Nothing you download is sent to the developer, and there is nowhere else for it to go. Deleting the app, or clearing its data from your phone's settings, removes everything.

The single exception is a diagnostic note when the app itself breaks, which carries none of your Hattrick data. It is described under When the app breaks.

What we never ask for

We never ask for your Hattrick login name or password, and we could not use them if you offered. Authorisation happens entirely through Hattrick's own OAuth flow: you sign in at Hattrick, Hattrick asks whether you want to grant this app access, and the app only ever receives an access token. This is required by clause 5.1 of the CHPP License Agreement.

What is stored, and where

Everything lives in the app's private storage on your device.

DataWhyHow long
Your CHPP access token and its secret To read your club data on your behalf Until you revoke access or clear the app's data
Your Hattrick UserID To notice if a different account authorises the app Same as above
The CHPP responses the app downloaded, as dated snapshots, compressed To show your club without downloading again, and to keep a record of your own data over time Until you clear the app's data

The access token is stored in the operating system's protected storage: on Android it is sealed with AES-256-GCM under a key that never leaves the Android Keystore; on iOS it lives in the Keychain with ThisDeviceOnly accessibility. Both are excluded from device backups on purpose, because a token restored onto a different phone would give that phone access to your Hattrick account.

The downloaded responses accumulate. A second download that is identical to the previous one does not add a snapshot. This history exists so that data about your own club is not lost, and nothing derived from it is shown to you today.

When the app breaks

When the app closes with an error, or when Hattrick sends a document this app cannot read, it writes a short diagnostic note in its own storage and sends it the next time you open the app. The note opens an issue in the public repository at github.com/jcmoro/hmclub/issues. This exists for one reason: so that a failure gets fixed instead of going unnoticed.

The note carries the class of the error, the first line of its message cut to 200 characters, the line of this app's own code where it happened, the app version, the Android version and the phone model.

It carries none of your Hattrick data. No team names, no player names, no fixtures, no amounts, no access token, and nothing that identifies you or your Hattrick account. The note says so in its own last line, so that anyone reading the issue can check.

These reports are public, because the repository where the app is fixed is public. GitHub, Inc. receives and stores them under its own terms.

The same failure is reported once. There is no way to turn the reports off from inside the app today; the author is the only user so far, and an off switch is owed before that stops being true.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with any other application, website or service. This is also required by clause 5.2 of the CHPP License Agreement.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics or any third-party tracker. The app measures nothing about how you use it. Its only network requests are to Hattrick's own CHPP endpoints and, when it breaks, to GitHub's issue API as described above.
  • We do not write anything back to Hattrick. The app is read-only.
  • We do not automate access to Hattrick. Every request follows something you did in the app.
  • We do not read another manager's private data. Any request for a team that is not yours is refused before it is sent.

Data about other people

Some of what Hattrick sends you names other people: your players, your staff, the opposing teams in your fixture list. That data arrives because you asked Hattrick for your own club, it stays on your device under the same terms as the rest, and it is never sent anywhere.

How you remove your data

Any of these is enough:

  • Revoke access in the app. The token is invalidated at Hattrick, deleted from the device, and everything downloaded is deleted with it.
  • Revoke access in Hattrick, under your CHPP settings. The next time the app checks, it finds the token rejected, deletes it and deletes everything downloaded.
  • Clear the app's data from your phone's settings, or uninstall the app. Nothing survives.

The app deliberately offers no storage management of its own: your phone already does that better, and duplicating it inside would add surface without adding capability.

If a different Hattrick account authorises the app, everything downloaded by the previous account is deleted first.

Children

The app is not directed at children. It requires a Hattrick account, and Hattrick's own terms govern who may hold one.

Changes to this policy

If what the app does with your data changes, this policy changes with it, and the date at the top changes too. The version in force ships with the app.
