Hattrick Manager Club

Your club on your phone.

Squad, skills, arena, economy, staff, training and fixtures in one place. Free, read-only, and nothing leaves your device.

Android · in development iOS · planned Read-only Free

What it looks like

Squad, player, club and matches. There is a dark theme of its own, designed rather than inverted: it follows your phone, or you fix it from inside the app.

What it shows

Squad
Every player with their current skills, age, form, experience, leadership, loyalty, specialty, injuries, cards, TSI and wage.
Arena
Capacity by section, and the expansion under way if there is one.
Economy
Cash, expected cash, and income, costs and balance for this week and the last.
Staff
Each member with their role, level and weekly cost.
Training
The settings in force, plus team spirit and confidence.
Fixtures
Matches played and matches to come.

Amounts arrive in your own currency. Dates are shown in Stockholm time, wherever your phone happens to be. The interface is available in English and Spanish.

How it works

There is no server

The app talks to Hattrick directly from your phone, signing its own requests with Hattrick's OAuth, and what it downloads stays on the device. Nothing is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

It never asks for your password

You authorise it at Hattrick, the same way as any other CHPP product, and you can revoke that access at any time — from inside the app or from your Hattrick settings. Revoking deletes everything the app had downloaded.

It is strictly read-only

No match orders, no lineups, no bids. It cannot change anything in your Hattrick account, because it never asked for permission to.

It keeps what it downloaded

So opening the app does not mean waiting for the network, and so the same request is not made twice.

Privacy

The short version: no server, no analytics, no advertising, no trackers, no third parties. The app makes no network request to any host other than Hattrick's own CHPP endpoints.
